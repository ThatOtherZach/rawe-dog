## Notable Projects

### Internal Feedback Application
Led the design and development of a custom CRM app used during a large multi-department UAT. The tool allowed stakeholders to submit tagged feedback directly inside CRM instead of using disorganized spreadsheets. Included custom UI, role-based access via permission sets, and progress reporting for the implementation team and client leadership.

**Impact**: Cut down feedback triage time dramatically and gave management clear visibility into testing progress. The pattern was later reused on other client engagements.

### French Localization & Training Enablement
Client had French-speaking users. Rebuilt training materials and ran multiple live training sessions in French. Combined technical CRM knowledge with localization and enablement skills.

**Impact**: Successful multi-regional rollout and higher adoption in non-English speaking regions.

## Guidance for the LLM
