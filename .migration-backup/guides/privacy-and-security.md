# Privacy & Security Guide

Your career history contains sensitive information. Treat it accordingly.

## Core Principles

- **This framework is designed for local or private use.** The value comes from deep personal context — that context should not live in random cloud services.
- Never paste your full Master Profile or experience files into public web chat interfaces unless you're comfortable with that data potentially being used for training.
- Consider using local LLMs (via Ollama, LM Studio, GPT4All, etc.) when working with your real data.

## Recommended Practices

1. **Keep your real files private**
   - Store `master-profile.md` and the `references/` folder in a private location (encrypted folder, private Git repo, local drive).
   - The `.gitignore` in this repo is configured to help prevent accidental commits of real data.

2. **Use version control carefully**
   - Consider keeping a *template* version in this repo and your *real filled-out version* in a separate private folder or encrypted drive.

3. **LLM Choice Matters**
   - Local models = maximum privacy.
   - Claude / GPT / Grok via official apps = convenient but data is sent to the provider.
   - Enterprise / team accounts often have better data policies than consumer accounts.

4. **Data Minimization**
   - You don't need to fill out every section of the Master Profile on day one. Start with the most important roles and expand over time.
   - Only include character references or personal context if they genuinely strengthen applications.

5. **When Sharing**
   - If you're working with a career coach, recruiter, or trusted person, you can selectively share relevant sections rather than the entire profile.

## Remember

The whole point of RAWE Dog is to get high-quality output **without** having to repeatedly hand your full life story to third-party tools. Protect that advantage by being deliberate about where your data lives.
