# Enterprise Business Systems Analyst Role — Work Experience Summary

## Role Summary
Highly technical services / enterprise environment. Focused on business systems analysis, requirements gathering, Jira workflow management, and complex data projects. Worked closely with stakeholders across multiple teams on large-scale system changes and data initiatives.

## Skills, Tools & Technology
Jira (advanced workflows, epics, reporting), Confluence, CRM/ERP (heavy configuration + some development), Excel + data analysis, Agile or Waterfall processes, stakeholder facilitation, test case design, UAT coordination.

## Key Projects & Impact

### Large-Scale Survey & Data Collection Platform
Led requirements and configuration for a complex internal survey platform with 1000+ questions and 4000+ responses. Designed intricate logic, validation rules, and reporting structures. Managed Jira epics and worked with developers on custom components.

**Challenges**: Highly complex conditional logic and stakeholder alignment across multiple business units.
**Outcome**: Delivered on time with high data quality. Created reusable patterns for future survey-style projects.

### Data Migration & Validation Work
Owned data mapping, cleansing, and validation for multiple system transitions. Custom Excel processes, built validation reports and worked with business users to resolve data issues pre-go-live.

**Impact**: Significantly reduced post-go-live data issues compared to previous migrations.

## Process & Collaboration Wins
- Improved Jira workflow hygiene and traceability between requirements, development, and QA.
- Created clearer documentation standards that reduced back-and-forth during handoffs.
- Regularly acted as the bridge between technical teams and business stakeholders.

## Guidance for the LLM
- Strong for BSA, requirements, enterprise process, Jira/SDLC, and data-heavy roles.
- Emphasize stakeholder management, complex logic, and data quality work when those skills are requested.
- This experience pairs well with more technical implementation experience when the job needs both analysis and delivery.
