# How to Build Your Master Profile (The Most Important Step)

The quality of everything RAWE Dog produces depends almost entirely on how well you build your `master-profile.md`.

## Why This Matters So Much

Generic AI resume tools fail because they don't have deep context about *you*. By investing time upfront to create a strong Master Profile + experience files, you give the LLM a reliable "source of truth" it can consult every single time.

## Recommended Approach

### 1. Start with the Tables (Highest Leverage)
- **Role-Type Alignment Table** — This is where you do your strategic thinking. Map which of your experiences best support different kinds of target roles.
- **Experience Keyword & Skill Index** — Helps the LLM quickly find the right material for specific requirements.

Spending time on these two tables will improve output quality more than almost anything else.

### 2. Be Honest About Strengths and Gaps
- Don't try to make every role sound equally strong for every type of job. The guidance columns exist so the LLM knows when to lead with something and when to use it lightly.
- It's okay (and useful) to note when certain experiences should be minimized for specific opportunities.

### 3. Tone Rules Are Powerful
- Clearly define how you want to sound. This prevents the LLM from defaulting to generic corporate language.
- Be specific about what to avoid (certain words, the dash character, etc.).

### 4. Experience Files > Generic Summaries
- The detailed experience files in `references/` are where the real power lives. Generic "I did X, Y, Z" summaries produce generic output.
- Use the STAR structure and include real metrics and project names where possible.

### 5. Iterate Over Time
- You don't need a perfect profile on day one. Start with your 2–3 most important roles and the alignment tables.
- Add more detail and new roles as you use the system and see where the output is weak.

## Pro Tips

- Write in your natural voice. The LLM will match it.
- Quantify wherever you can (even rough estimates are better than nothing).
- If you have strong character references with good quotes, include 1–2 short ones — they can be powerful in cover letters when they fit.
- Review the full example in `examples/full-example/` to see what a well-built profile looks like.

The upfront work is real, but once it's done, tailoring resumes becomes dramatically faster and higher quality.
