# RAWE Dog — Tailoring Workflow

Use this when the user wants to tailor a resume or documents for a specific job.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Read the full Master Profile** first.
2. **Analyze the job posting** for:
   - Required skills and keywords
   - Responsibilities and level
   - Industry/domain signals
   - Any red flags or strong preferences
3. **Consult the Role-Type Alignment Table** and **Experience Keyword Index** to decide which 1–3 experiences should lead and which should support.
4. **Apply the Workplace Usage Guidance** (when to emphasize vs minimize each role).
5. **Pull specific, relevant content** from the detailed experience files (projects, metrics, STAR stories).
6. **Apply tone and formatting rules** strictly.
7. **Generate the document** in clean markdown.
8. **Offer next steps** (tweaks, cover letter, STAR prep, save to tracker).

"Here's a job posting for a Senior Salesforce Consultant role. Tailor my resume for it."

## Pro Tips for Best Results

- The more specific the job posting, the better the tailoring.
- If the user has many roles, be ruthless about only including what strengthens the application for *this* opportunity.
- When using independent/side work, frame it professionally as client deliverables.
- Always prioritize real, quantified impact over trying to sound impressive.
